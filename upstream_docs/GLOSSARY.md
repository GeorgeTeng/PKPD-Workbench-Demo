# Translational PK/PD research requirements

## Language

**PK translation**:
Evidence-supported extrapolation of accepted PK models across species or populations, with predictions, uncertainty and independent validation under the project's data-access conditions.
_Avoid_: Human PK as a module name

**Human PK data**:
Pharmacokinetic observations or reported parameters from humans, whose permitted use depends on the project's access and validation rules.

**Project objective**:
The scientific question the project intends to answer, including its intended use and study scope.

**Project requirement**:
An explicit obligation authorized for this project, with its original conditions, exceptions and applicability.
_Avoid_: Treating every literature method as a project requirement

**Literature fact**:
A source-bound statement about what a study did or observed. It does not impose an obligation on another project.

**Candidate suggestion**:
A proposed method or decision that has not been adopted as a project obligation.

**Processing rule**:
A rule governing how evidence and requirements are extracted, checked and communicated. It does not by itself add scientific obligations to a project.

**Source authority**:
The permission of a source to establish project obligations. Permission to read a source does not necessarily grant this authority.

**Published project rules**:
A reviewed collection of explicit project obligations whose source, conditions and scope remain traceable.
