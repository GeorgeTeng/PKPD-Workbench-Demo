# Animal-to-human PK translation: demonstration brief

This is an assistant-compiled demonstration of the current conversation, not a signed proposal or a record of completed experiments.

Objective: Plan prediction of human PK and, where evidence permits, exposure-response from animal or in vitro evidence. Assess model reliability, species selection and per-species sample-size requirements.

Antibacterials are the main focus. Polymyxin B and colistin are candidate drugs; the final compound is not selected. Apramycin is the preferred translational project template.

Administration route and dosing schedule are not fixed. The destination is humans; the particular target population is not selected. Eligible input systems are animal PK and in vitro disposition studies. The supplied materials do not specify measurement units, concentration basis, species inventory or animal numbers.

Available materials consist of literature reviews, public-material inventories and statistical notes. No project-level animal concentration observations have been supplied. Public papers and published summaries do not establish possession of individual observations or runnable estimation code.

Do not use PBPK. At the prediction stage assume there is no drug-specific human PK dataset. If human PK becomes available, reserve it for evaluation after freezing predictions. Historical scaling methods calibrated using other drugs' human PK must be identified as such.

Compare species choices and sampling designs rather than assuming that more species must be better. Plan sample size around an explicit estimand, acceptable confidence-interval precision, variability and sampling structure. No numerical precision threshold is yet fixed.

Preserve known values, units, total/free basis, negation and conditional scope. Keep unknown information as NOT_FOUND. Separate project facts, external evidence and proposed methods. Raw-data cleaning and quality control belong to B2, after this context/planning stage.

The requested output is a reviewable research workflow with data requirements and explicit uncertainties. Human exposure-response is conditional on suitable pharmacodynamic evidence; no clinical efficacy validation is claimed.
